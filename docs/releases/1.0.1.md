# HyperMusicCover-Enhanced 1.0.1

Changes since [v1.0.0](https://github.com/Lewewe/HyperMusicCover-Enhanced/releases/tag/v1.0.0).

## ✨ New

- **Visualizer bar colors:** artwork gradient, white, dark gray or a custom color.
- **Keep music artwork collapsed:** optional, so music no longer opens big cover art by itself.
- **Inactive lyrics blur:** adjustable from 0% to 200%.
- **Full clock over the media player:** with only the player open, the clock keeps its date and weather when there is room.

## 🕐 Clock faces

- More clock faces shrink correctly over big cover art and lyrics (eastern, rhombus, magazine, oriental and others).
- Magazine faces are no longer cut off, and clocks no longer grow too large on the AOD.
- Fixed the tall clock style staying full size over big cover art.
- The clock stays readable over the music background on bright wallpapers.

## 🛠 Fixes

- **Use wallpaper background:** live wallpapers now show, and the dim stays at 0% lyrics blur.
- Better balanced visualizer, with mids and highs more visible.
- The Super Island wave follows the chosen bar color.
- Lock-screen notification islands show the right picture, including HyperIsland ones.
- KuGou artwork no longer stays blurred after a track change.
- The Lyrics button in full-screen cover works both ways.

Tested on Xiaomi 17 Pro with HyperOS 4. Requires root, LSPosed (modern API) and Android 15+.
