# HyperMusicCover-Enhanced 1.1.0

Changes since [v1.0.1](https://github.com/Lewewe/HyperMusicCover-Enhanced/releases/tag/v1.0.1).

## ✨ New

- **StandBy (alpha):** a full-screen clock, music and widgets while the phone charges on its side. Download the optional [HyperImmersive](https://github.com/Lewewe/HyperImmersive) extension from the Extensions page.
- **ColorOS-style lock screen pill:** a capsule that grows into the notification list under your finger.
- **iPhone-ify (A/B):** hold Super Island to open an iPhone-style card at the camera, over the other pills, for music and other islands.
- **Moving backdrop:** artwork colours drift behind the big cover, as a gradient or a mesh gradient, with a switch to turn it off.
- **Updates from Home:** a card downloads and installs a new release, then restarts the scope when root is granted.
- **Extras page:** a switch for the depth effect limits.
- **New app icon**, drawn on a mesh gradient.

## 🎵 Music and lyrics

- The visualizer moves like iPhone's.
- The kaomoji is shorter and cuter, flips on real kicks and bass hits, and dozes off when the beat stops.
- The big cover flips on a track change, including when a song ends on its own.
- The next lyric line peeks in under a long one.
- The Lyrics button grows in place, with a new icon.
- The lock screen pill follows HyperOS Soft Glass.

## 🎬 Spotify Canvas

- The big cover stays when a Canvas track starts, and the clock takes its colour from the artwork.
- The video wallpaper plays from the start when the cover gives it back, and comes back right away when the player is minimized.
- The Canvas backdrop is released when the Spotify session ends on the lock screen.
- If an extension can't be downloaded, its button opens its GitHub releases instead.

## 🛠 Fixes

- Adapted to stable HyperOS 4 notifications.
- Fixed the tiny clock over the big cover.
- The big cover stays on the AOD after an unlocked sleep, and after the list covered the player.
- Fixed a System UI crash when changing songs right before the AOD.
- The lock screen no longer flickers on wake.
- Opening the shade, island or lock screen no longer restarts the audio capture.
- Spotify Connect casting is detected, and the island and output button keep their native animation while casting.
- Non-square covers land where they rest when expanding from the player.
- The big cover fades out under the PIN pad.
- A tap on a notification no longer puts the cover back.
- The fingerprint icon goes away after a face unlock.
- The notification count shows as n+, and island text can stop scrolling.
- Removed the notification shade's flowing light.

Thanks to puhboo for the Canvas, visualizer and kaomoji work in this release.

Tested on Xiaomi 17 Pro with HyperOS 4. Requires root, LSPosed (modern API) and Android 15+.
