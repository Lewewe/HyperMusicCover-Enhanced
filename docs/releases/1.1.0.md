# HyperMusicCover-Enhanced 1.1.0

## English

Changes since [1.0.1-rev01](https://github.com/Lewewe/HyperMusicCover-Enhanced/releases/tag/v1.0.1).

### ✨ New

- **StandBy (alpha):** a full-screen clock, music and widgets while the phone charges on its side, through the optional [HyperImmersive](https://github.com/Lewewe/HyperImmersive) extension. Set it up from the Extensions page.
- **ColorOS-style lock screen pill:** now the default. Pull it up and it grows into the notification list under your finger. Island style switches back to HyperOS.
- **iPhone-ify (A/B):** hold Super Island to open an iPhone-style card at the camera, for music and other islands.
- **Moving background colours:** the artwork's colours drift behind the big cover, as a gradient or a mesh gradient.
- **Updates from Home:** a card shows a new release and installs it. With root it also restarts the scope.

### 🎵 Music and lyrics

- The visualizer moves like iPhone's.
- The kaomoji is reworked: new faces, flips on real kicks and bass hits, and dozes off when the beat stops.
- New option: flip the big cover when the track changes.
- The music pill and big cover follow your finger in and out of the player and lyrics.
- The next lyric line peeks in under a long one, and the Lyrics button has a new icon.

### 🔔 Lock screen and notifications

- Expanded island theme: Default or OLED.
- The lock screen pill follows HyperOS Soft Glass.
- New options: remove the fingerprint after a face unlock, open the list without the media player, stop long text scrolling in the pill.
- The notification count shows as N+ at the pill's end.

### 🎬 Canvas and extensions

- The big cover stays when a Canvas track starts, and the clock takes its colour from the artwork.
- If an extension can't be downloaded, its button opens its GitHub releases.

### 🎨 Also new

- New app icon.
- An Extras page with a switch for the depth effect limits.
- Ko-fi on the donate page.

### 🛠 Fixes

- Adapted to stable HyperOS 4 notifications.
- Fixed a System UI crash when changing songs right before the AOD.
- The lock screen no longer flickers on wake.
- Fixed the tiny clock over the big cover.
- The big cover stays on the AOD after the list covered the player, and keeps its size after an unlock.
- Video wallpapers no longer hide Canvas and come back properly from the cover.
- Opening the shade, island or lock screen no longer restarts the audio capture.
- Spotify Connect casting is detected, and the island keeps its native animation while casting.

Thanks to puhboo for the Canvas, visualizer and kaomoji work in this release.

Tested on Xiaomi 17 Pro with HyperOS 4. Requires root, LSPosed (modern API) and Android 15+.

## 简体中文

自 [1.0.1-rev01](https://github.com/Lewewe/HyperMusicCover-Enhanced/releases/tag/v1.0.1) 以来的变更。

### ✨ 新功能

- **StandBy（Alpha）：** 手机横放充电时显示全屏时钟、音乐和小组件，通过可选扩展 [HyperImmersive](https://github.com/Lewewe/HyperImmersive) 提供，可在“扩展”页面完成设置。
- **ColorOS 风格锁屏胶囊：** 现为默认样式。上拉时胶囊跟随手指展开为通知列表，可在“岛样式”中切换回 HyperOS。
- **iPhone-ify（A/B）：** 长按超级岛，在摄像头处打开 iPhone 风格卡片，音乐和其他超级岛均适用。
- **流动背景：** 封面颜色在大封面后缓慢流动，可选渐变或网格渐变。
- **首页更新：** 首页卡片提示新版本并直接安装；有 root 时还会重启作用域。

### 🎵 音乐与歌词

- 音频可视化的动态与 iPhone 相似。
- 颜文字全面改进：新表情，随真实鼓点和低音翻转，节拍停止后打瞌睡。
- 新选项：切歌时翻转大封面。
- 音乐胶囊和大封面进出播放器、歌词时跟随手指。
- 长句下方可露出下一行歌词；歌词按钮换用新图标。

### 🔔 锁屏与通知

- 展开超级岛主题：默认或 OLED。
- 锁屏胶囊跟随 HyperOS 柔光玻璃效果。
- 新选项：人脸解锁后隐藏指纹图标、打开通知列表时不显示媒体播放器、关闭胶囊内长文本滚动。
- 通知数量以 N+ 显示在胶囊末端。

### 🎬 Canvas 与扩展

- Canvas 歌曲开始播放时保留大封面，时钟颜色取自封面。
- 扩展无法下载时，按钮会改为打开其 GitHub 发布页。

### 🎨 其他

- 全新应用图标。
- 新增“更多功能”页面，含景深效果限制开关。
- 捐赠页面新增 Ko-fi。

### 🛠 修复

- 适配 HyperOS 4 稳定版通知。
- 修复息屏前切歌导致 System UI 崩溃的问题。
- 修复唤醒时锁屏闪烁的问题。
- 修复大封面上时钟过小的问题。
- 通知列表覆盖播放器后，大封面仍保留在息屏显示上；解锁后尺寸保持不变。
- 视频壁纸不再遮挡 Canvas，并能从大封面正常恢复。
- 打开通知栏、超级岛或锁屏时不再重启音频捕获。
- 可识别 Spotify Connect 投放，投放时超级岛保留原生动画。

感谢 puhboo 为本版本贡献 Canvas、音频可视化和颜文字相关工作。

已在小米 17 Pro（HyperOS 4）上测试。需要 root、LSPosed（现代 API）和 Android 15 及以上。
