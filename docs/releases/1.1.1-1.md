## English

Changes since [1.1.1](https://github.com/Lewewe/HyperMusicCover-Enhanced/releases/tag/v1.1.1).

### 🎵 Music and lyrics

- **Full-screen cover:** moving between the big cover and the media player now scales, fades and blurs in place.
- The Flip the cover on track change switch is grayed out with the full-screen cover, where it has no effect.

### 🛠 Fixes

- With the full-screen cover and Use wallpaper background off, closing the lyrics no longer leaves the media player without a cover or thumbnail.
- The title no longer jumps when the big cover shrinks into the media player.

Tested on Xiaomi 17 Pro with HyperOS 4. Requires root, LSPosed (modern API) and Android 15+.

## 简体中文

自 [1.1.1](https://github.com/Lewewe/HyperMusicCover-Enhanced/releases/tag/v1.1.1) 以来的变更。

### 🎵 音乐与歌词

- **全屏封面：** 大封面与媒体播放器之间切换时，改为在原位缩放、淡入淡出并模糊。
- 使用全屏封面时，“切歌时翻转封面”开关会变灰，因为它在此模式下不起作用。

### 🛠 修复

- 修复使用全屏封面并关闭“使用锁屏壁纸背景”时，关闭歌词后媒体播放器没有封面和缩略图的问题。
- 修复大封面缩小到媒体播放器时标题跳动的问题。

已在小米 17 Pro（HyperOS 4）上测试。需要 root、LSPosed（现代 API）和 Android 15 及以上。
