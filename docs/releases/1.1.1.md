## English

Changes since [1.1.0](https://github.com/Lewewe/HyperMusicCover-Enhanced/releases/tag/v1.1.0).

### ✨ New

- **Long press to switch lyrics:** on the media player, long press the cover art to hide the lyrics, and again to bring them back.
- **Full-size buttons (Beta):** a new switch for the ColorOS pill puts the torch and camera back at 100%. The pill is as tall as they are and shorter to fit.
- **Changelog on the update card:** a button on the Home update card shows what's new before you install.
- **Small updates:** the update card can now offer tags like `v1.1.1-1`, so small fixes reach you in the app.

### 🎵 Music and lyrics

- The Lyrics button now only shows on the big cover, so it no longer covers other apps' extra controls on the media player. This also removes searching for lyrics from the companion page for now.
- The Lyrics button shrinks and grows from its own spot instead of sliding in with the title.
- Lines already sung stay above a long lyric line instead of leaving the space empty.

### 🔔 Lock screen and notifications

- The notification count shows as N+ even when notification content is hidden on the lock screen.

### 🛠 Fixes

- The title no longer jumps when moving to a song without lyrics.
- The update card shrinks when pressed instead of tilting.

Tested on Xiaomi 17 Pro with HyperOS 4. Requires root, LSPosed (modern API) and Android 15+.

## 简体中文

自 [1.1.0](https://github.com/Lewewe/HyperMusicCover-Enhanced/releases/tag/v1.1.0) 以来的变更。

### ✨ 新功能

- **长按切换歌词：** 在媒体播放器上长按封面可隐藏歌词，再次长按可重新显示。
- **全尺寸按钮（Beta）：** ColorOS 胶囊新增开关，可将手电筒和相机按钮恢复为 100%，胶囊同样变高，并缩短长度以适应。
- **更新卡片显示更新日志：** 首页更新卡片新增按钮，安装前即可查看更新内容。
- **小版本更新：** 更新卡片现在可以推送 `v1.1.1-1` 这样的版本，小修复也能在应用内收到。

### 🎵 音乐与歌词

- 歌词按钮现在只在大封面上显示，不再遮挡其他应用在媒体播放器上的额外按钮。因此，暂时无法在颜文字页面搜索歌词。
- 歌词按钮在原位缩放显示和隐藏，不再随标题滑入。
- 过长的歌词上方会继续显示已唱过的歌词，不再留白。

### 🔔 锁屏与通知

- 锁屏隐藏通知内容时，通知数量同样以 N+ 显示。

### 🛠 修复

- 修复切换到无歌词歌曲时标题跳动的问题。
- 更新卡片按下时改为缩小，不再倾斜。

已在小米 17 Pro（HyperOS 4）上测试。需要 root、LSPosed（现代 API）和 Android 15 及以上。
